ResumeFit — Product Requirements Document (PRD)

1. Overview

ResumeFit is a web app that gives students an instant ATS (Applicant Tracking System) compatibility score for their resume, along with AI-generated suggestions to improve it — free for a quick check, paid for deeper editing help. Built via Antigravity as a group project.

2. Problem statement

Many student resumes get filtered out by ATS software before a human ever reads them, but most students have no way to check ATS-compatibility or know exactly what to fix. Existing tools are either generic checkers that give a vague score, or expensive career-coaching services.

3. Target users

  • University/MBA students applying for internships and full-time roles
  • Primary use case: a quick resume check before submitting an application — either for a Data Analyst, Consultant, Marketing, or HR role, or against any other job via a pasted JD

4. Goals

  • Give an accurate, explainable ATS score in under a minute
  • Convert free users to paying subscribers via genuinely useful gated features, not artificial restriction
  • Ship a working prototype first, validate scoring quality, then layer in monetization

5. Success metrics (draft — refine with your team)

  • Number of resumes scanned
  • % of free users who open/scroll to the locked recommendations (interest signal)
  • Free-to-paid conversion rate
  • Average ATS score improvement between a user's first and second scan

6. Scope by phase

Phase 1 — Prototype (build now)

  • Upload a resume (PDF/DOCX)
  • Choose: general ATS score / preset role JD (Data Analyst, Consultant, Marketing, HR) / paste a custom JD
  • Agentic scoring pipeline → numeric score + category breakdown
  • Top 3 recommendations shown free; remainder shown as locked/blurred (no payment wired yet)
  • Sky-blue, calming UI

Phase 2 — Free product (post-prototype polish)

  • Same flow, refined UI/UX
  • Clear error handling for unreadable files (scanned/image-based PDFs)
  • Possibly resume history within a session

Phase 3 — Paid tier

  • Monthly subscription: unlimited scans and re-scans
  • One-click "generate updated resume" with suggested edits applied, downloadable as DOCX/PDF
  • Upload a reference/college template → resume restructured to match it
  • Payment integration (gateway TBD)

7. Functional requirements

Feature Tier Requirement
Resume upload Free Accept PDF and DOCX; clearly flag unreadable (scanned/image) files
Scoring mode Free General score, one of 4 preset JDs, or a pasted custom JD
ATS score Free 0–100 score with a breakdown by keyword match, formatting, sections, achievements
Recommendations (top 3) Free Shown in full
Recommendations (remaining) Paid Unlocked via subscription
Unlimited scans Paid No monthly cap on scans/re-scans
Resume auto-edit + download Paid Apply suggested changes, export as DOCX/PDF
Template matching Paid Restructure resume to match an uploaded reference/template

8. Non-functional requirements

  • Design: sky-blue palette (#38BDF8 primary / #0284C7 accent / #F0F9FF background), calm and uncluttered — avoid a dense dashboard feel
  • Scoring runs as an agentic pipeline (parse → analyze JD → score → recommend), not a single opaque prompt, so each step's output is inspectable and debuggable
  • Prototype has no login, database, or payment processing — those arrive in Phase 3

9. Out of scope (for now)

  • Payment gateway integration
  • User accounts / login
  • Resume storage beyond a single session
  • File formats other than PDF/DOCX
  • Any deliberately fake or randomized scoring

10. Open questions

  • Subscription price point and billing frequency
  • Payment gateway (Razorpay, Stripe, etc.)
  • Whether preset JDs are static text or refreshed periodically
  • Whether template matching should preserve the reference's exact formatting, or just its section order and style
  • Team roles/responsibilities for the build
